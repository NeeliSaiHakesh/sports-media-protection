How It Works

From upload to legal notice in under 3 seconds

1

Upload Your Media

Drag and drop images directly. We support JPEG, PNG, and WebP formats. Batch uploads supported.

2

AI Fingerprinting

Our perceptual hash engine normalizes the image and generates a 64-bit visual fingerprint that's resistant to minor edits.

3

Database Comparison

The fingerprint is compared against our entire reference database. Hamming distance is converted to a similarity percentage.

4

Instant Verdict + Risk Score

The system delivers a clear decision: Original, Suspicious, or Copied — with a platform-weighted risk score.

5

Generate Legal Notice

With one click, generate a professionally formatted DMCA takedown notice ready to submit.
